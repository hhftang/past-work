# past-work

A collection of scripts from past projects, including:

- **PhD:** R and Python scripts for ML clustering, genomic, and microbiome analyses, as part of a project investigating heterogeneity of childhood asthma. The results of this project have been published in:
    - [Teo et al 2018. Airway Microbiota Dynamics Uncover a Critical Window for Interplay of Pathogenic Bacteria and Allergy in Childhood Respiratory Disease.](ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/30212648)
    - [Tang et al 2018. Trajectories of childhood immune development and respiratory health relevant to asthma and allergy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30320550/)
    - [Tang et al 2021. Developmental patterns in the nasopharyngeal microbiome during infancy are associated with asthma risk](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33091409/)
- **Baker-Cambridge:** selected scripts from polygenic risk score calculation and simulated power calculations for host-viral interaction analysis (to be uploaded).
- **Cancer Council Victoria:** selected R and SAS scripts from the DCPP obesity and stomach cancer project, and the MCCS GI lymphoma project (to be uploaded). The results of this project will be published in Lancet Oncology as:
    - Tang et al 2026. Obesity and risk of stomach cancer and its subtypes: a pooled analysis of two million adults.

These scripts serve as a record and a representation of the type of work I have done in the past. As such they may not necessarily represent best practice nor how I will approach the same tasks in the present day.

In most cases the scripts will not run as they have been detached from original raw data, in compliance with private and personal information protections.
